
# Introduction {.unnumbered}

The Noble Quran is the Speech of Allāh. This is its foremost and intrinsic
distinction. Its second distinction is that the task of conveying, explaining,
and reminding people of its teachings was entrusted to the noble Prophet
Muḥammad \hon{\PBUH}{\BODY}, the most eminent of all Messengers. The majesty and
loftiness of these two associations demanded that the Noble Quran become the
focal point of our attention, and that, through every rise and fall of life, its
guidance serve as the illuminating light by which we chart our course.

Yet it is a matter of profound regret that a large proportion of Muslims remain
unfamiliar with the meanings and teachings of this sacred Scripture. More
distressing still is that they scarcely feel any sorrow over this estrangement
and neglect. It does not even occur to them that the copies of the Noble Quran
resting upon their desks, arranged on their shelves, or placed upon the niches
of their homes carry within them a message, contain solutions to the problems of
human life, and stand as the heralds and callers to a unique and transformative
invitation.

There is another group whose members have fashioned their own conception of
Islam. They possess self-constructed frameworks and preconceived intellectual
models. They seek to interpret the _āyāt_ of the Quran through the lens of these
assumptions, and wish to organize the Islamic way of life according to these
very frameworks and designs. In short, they do not wish to make their thinking
subservient to the Quran. Rather, they seek to make the Quran conform to their
own thinking.

Both of these groups are truly deprived. They ought to reflect that the Noble
Quran, from which we have turned away through our own misfortune, or which we
have attempted to understand through unsound methods, is in reality the very
elixir whose influence transformed highway robbers and brigands into the trusted
custodians and confidants of the secrets brought by Jibrīl \hon{\S_HIM}{\BODY}.
It is that priceless treasure which, once attained, caused the jewels and
precious gems of this world to appear no more valuable than shards of broken
pottery in the eyes of the desert dwellers. It is that incomparable treasury of
knowledge and wisdom before whose guidance even the greatest intellects of
history found themselves compelled to determine the course of their lives. In
the presence of its profound wisdom, they discovered delight in acknowledging
the limitations and defeat of their own intellect and reason.

It is therefore essential that the direction of the Muslims be turned once again
towards this true _qiblah_ of aspiration, that this weary and wayworn caravan be
led once more towards the Sacred Sanctuary, and that, through the mediation of
this blessed heavenly Scripture, our dormant fortune be awakened anew.

The primary purpose of the pages that follow is precisely this: to encourage
Muslims to derive benefit from the Noble Quran, to make them aware of the
obstacles that lie along this path, and to explain the method by which the
desired objective may be attained and the intended destination reached.

After reading these observations, one might suppose that I have imposed
excessive restrictions and conditions upon benefiting from the Noble Quran.
However, Allāh knows best that my sole intention is to uphold the truth and
offer sincere counsel. Before us lie the pages of more than thirteen and a half
centuries of Muslim history. These pages clearly demonstrate how those lacking
proper understanding attempted to employ the Quranic _āyāt_ in support of their
own aims and objectives. Rather than striving to draw themselves nearer to the
Quran, they sought instead to draw the Quran nearer to their own ideas and
preconceptions.

It may be stated with complete confidence that whenever individuals, whether
intentionally or unintentionally, fell into error in this regard, the principal
reason was their failure to observe the principles and conditions necessary for
understanding the Quran. The inevitable consequence was that each person came to
regard his own opinion as being established by the Quran, and became fully
satisfied with that conclusion. Whoever wishes may consult _Ḥujaj al-Quran_ by
Imam Fakhr al-Dīn al-Rāzī \hon{\M_HIM}{\BODY}, together with the literature of
ʿIlm al-Kalām, and will find confirmation of what we have stated.

It therefore seemed appropriate to indicate the correct path to those who
sincerely seek the truth. By adopting this path---_in shāʾ Allāh_---one may hope
for the unveiling of the secrets of the Divine Speech and aspire to attain its
true objective.

Allāh alone is the Granter of success.
