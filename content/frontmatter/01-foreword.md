In the Name of Allāh, the Most Compassionate, the Most Merciful

Among the graduates of Nadwat al-ʿUlamāʾ who attained distinction in the
sciences of the Quran, the esteemed Shaykh Muḥammad Owais al-Nagrāmī al-Nadwī
\hon{\M_HIM}{\BODY} occupies a particularly eminent position.

A scholar as encyclopaedic as Sayyid al-Ṭāʾifah, ʿAllāmah Sayyid Sulaymān
al-Nadwī \hon{\M_HIM}{\BODY}, emerges only once in centuries. Although he is
widely recognised as an outstanding biographer of the Prophet
\hon{\PBUH}{\BODY}, the reality is that his principal field of devotion was the
Noble Quran. Allāh had bestowed upon him an exceptional aptitude for
understanding the Quran---a gift granted to only a select few. Shaykh Muḥammad
Owais al-Nagrāmī al-Nadwī \hon{\M_HIM}{\BODY} benefited from Sayyid Ṣāḥib
\hon{\M_HIM}{\BODY} through close and specialised study and, in the sciences of
the Quran, was regarded as his true successor.

Among the Shaykh's scholarly contributions, Tafsīr al-Qayyim stands as a
masterpiece. In this work, he gathered the exegetical discussions scattered
throughout the writings of ʿAllāmah Ibn al-Qayyim \hon{\M_HIM}{\BODY} into a
single volume, thereby producing what effectively became an independent
commentary on the Quran. Throughout his life, he served as the foremost teacher
of Tafsīr at Nadwat al-ʿUlamāʾ and authored numerous works in this field.

The present book 'How To Study The Qurʾan?: 12 Principles for Understanding the
Qur'an' addresses a subject of exceptional importance and benefit. It sheds
light on the principles and guidelines essential for understanding the Quran.
Among the foundational works on the principles of Tafsīr is Al-Fawz al-Kabīr fī
Uṣūl al-Tafsīr, while Imam Shāh Walī Allāh al-Dihlawī \hon{\M_HIM}{\BODY} also
authored an independent treatise explaining the principles governing the
translation of the meanings of the Noble Quran. In the present work, Shaykh
Muḥammad Owais al-Nagrāmī al-Nadwī \hon{\M_HIM}{\BODY} discusses the
prerequisites for understanding the Quran and elaborates upon the fundamental
principles governing this discipline.

Shaykh Owais \hon{\M_HIM}{\BODY} belonged to the distinguished scholarly family
of Nagrām, a family that produced many great scholars, authors, and reformers
for the Ummah. Just as he was an eminent scholar, author, and teacher, he was
also an accomplished reformer and spiritual guide. Through his efforts, a vast
region was illuminated with the light of knowledge and religion.

It is therefore a matter of great pleasure that the Shaykh's great-grandson,
Moḥammad Zahid ibn Ṭāriq, has undertaken the translation and publication of this
valuable treatise in English. Bringing the Shaykh's works to a wider readership
is an important contribution towards preserving and transmitting his scholarly
legacy, particularly as many of his writings remain accessible primarily to
readers of Urdu or Arabic. It is especially heartening to see a member of his
own family undertake this responsibility and thereby help introduce the Shaykh's
scholarship to a new generation of readers.

The work truly exemplifies the saying:

I am also pleased that this English translation follows soon after the
publication of the carefully checked Urdu edition in early 2026 by another of
the Shaykh's family, Shaykh Faiẓan al-Nagrāmī al-Nadwī. It is hoped that this
work will prove especially beneficial to students and researchers engaged in the
study of the sciences of the Quran, and that it will encourage further efforts
to preserve and bring to light the valuable writings of Shaykh Owais
\hon{\M_HIM}{\BODY}.

May Allāh Most High accept this effort, make it a means of benefit for its
readers, and grant it lasting acceptance and benefit for generations to come.
Āmīn.
