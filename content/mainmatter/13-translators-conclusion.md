
# Translator's Conclusion {.unnumbered}

The discussion that has preceded makes one thing clear: the treasures of the
Quran are not attained merely by reading its words or knowing the meanings of
its vocabulary. It is the Kalām Allāh, the final revelation sent for the
guidance of mankind. Its proper understanding therefore requires a heart that
recognises its majesty, a mind that approaches it with humility, and a method
grounded in the Quran itself, the Sunnah of the Messenger of Allāh
\hon{\PBUH}{\BODY}, and the understanding of the earliest generations.

The first requirement is reverence for the Quran. One who does not recognise the
greatness of the One whose speech it is cannot hope to appreciate the greatness
of His speech. The Quran opens its treasures to those who approach it with
_taqwá_, sincerity, attentiveness, and a genuine desire for guidance. It is not
merely a subject of intellectual study, but a book of guidance, purification,
admonition, and reform.

Alongside this reverence must be a firm attachment to the Messenger of Allāh
\hon{\PBUH}{\BODY}. The Quran was revealed through him, taught by him, explained
by him, and embodied in his life. His Sunnah is therefore inseparable from the
proper understanding of the Quran. Whoever seeks to understand the Book while
turning away from the guidance of the one to whom it was revealed is attempting
to walk a road without its appointed guide.

The student of the Quran must also possess a sincere desire to benefit from it.
The Quran is \ITA{هُدًى لِلْمُتَّقِين}, guidance for those who are conscious of
Allāh. The question is not merely whether one can read the Quran, but whether
one approaches it with the willingness to be changed by it.

The journey of understanding the Quran is not completed in a day. If Arabic is
lacking, let its study begin. If its meanings are not understood, let it be read
with a reliable translation and _tafsīr_. If something remains unclear, let those
of knowledge be consulted. What matters is that the journey begins.

The ultimate purpose is not merely to know what the Quran says, but to allow the
Quran to reform what we are. Its verses should correct our beliefs, purify our
hearts, regulate our conduct, and direct our lives towards the pleasure of
Allāh. Let it become not merely a book that we read, but the Book by which we
live.

Begin today. Read the Quran, learn it, reflect upon it, and act upon its
guidance. If we establish a true relationship with the Book of Allāh, it will
illuminate our hearts, reform our lives, and, by the permission of Allāh, become
a means of our success in this world and the Hereafter.

May Allāh grant us a true understanding of His Book, make the Quran the light of
our hearts and the guide of our lives, and enable us to fulfil the rights of
this magnificent revelation.
