
# Following the Messenger \hon{\PBUH}{\HEADER}

Just as Allāh, Blessed and Exalted, clothed His attribute of Speech in the
Arabic language, named it the Noble Quran, and made it the means of
communication between Himself and His servants, He likewise made the blessed
person of Muḥammad \hon{\PBUH}{\BODY} the source of illumination for explaining,
expounding, and practically demonstrating the Quran. Therefore, anyone who
wishes to derive benefit from the Noble Quran must possess, with complete
openness of heart, unwavering trust in, adherence to, and submission before this
noble Messenger \hon{\PBUH}{\BODY}. To expect to benefit from the Quran without
this indispensable intermediary is an exercise in futility.

The Noble Quran itself explains the relationship between the Quran and the
Messenger \hon{\PBUH}{\BODY}. This is a clear proof that attachment to the mantle of
Prophethood is essential for anyone seeking to benefit from the Noble Quran.
Allāh Most High says,

\AYAH_AR_START
يَتۡلُواْ عَلَيۡهِمۡ ءَايَٰتِهِۦ وَيُزَكِّيهِمۡ وَيُعَلِّمُهُمُ ٱلۡكِتَٰبَ وَٱلۡحِكۡمَةَ
\AYAH_AR_END

\AYAH_START
He recites His verses to them, purifies them, and teaches them the Book and
wisdom. (Sūrat al-Jumuʿah, Q62:2)
\AYAH_END

It is evident from this that teaching the Book of Allāh formed an integral part
of the Prophet's \hon{\PBUH}{\BODY} mission. Elsewhere, Allāh says,

\AYAH_AR_START
وَأَنزَلۡنَآ إِلَيۡكَ ٱلذِّكۡرَ لِتُبَيِّنَ لِلنَّاسِ مَا نُزِّلَ إِلَيۡهِمۡ وَلَعَلَّهُمۡ يَتَفَكَّرُونَ ٤٤
\AYAH_AR_END

\AYAH_START
And We have revealed to you the Reminder so that you may explain to the people
what has been sent down to them, and so that they may reflect. (Sūrat al-Naḥl,
Q16:44)
\AYAH_END

The meaning is that the responsibility for teaching, explaining, and expounding
the Noble Quran was entrusted by Allāh to the Prophet \hon{\PBUH}{\BODY}.
Consequently, to separate oneself from the light of Prophethood and then attempt
to reflect upon the Quran, expecting to derive benefit from its teachings, is
nothing but stepping into darkness.

For this reason, when a man said to Muṭarrif ibn ʿAbdillāh ibn al-Shikhkhīr
\hon{\M_HIM}{\BODY}, "Relate to us something other than the Quran," he
replied, "By Allāh, we seek no substitute for the Quran. Rather, alongside the
Quran, we seek the one who possessed the greatest understanding of it," meaning
the Messenger of Allāh \hon{\PBUH}{\BODY}.[-@@jami3-bayan-ilm, vol. 2, p. 1193, #2349]

Imam al-Shāṭibī \hon{\M_HIM}{\BODY} writes, "...the Sunnah serves as an
explanation and commentary on the meanings and rulings of the Book [of
Allāh]..."[-@@muwafaqat, vol. 4, p. 878]

This Prophetic Sunnah is itself among the great favors of Allāh, counted in the
Noble Quran as one of His special blessings upon the believers. Allāh Most
High says,

\AYAH_AR_START
لَقَدۡ مَنَّ ٱللَّهُ عَلَى ٱلۡمُؤۡمِنِينَ إِذۡ بَعَثَ فِيهِمۡ رَسُولٗا مِّنۡ أَنفُسِهِمۡ يَتۡلُواْ عَلَيۡهِمۡ ءَايَٰتِهِۦ وَيُزَكِّيهِمۡ وَيُعَلِّمُهُمُ ٱلۡكِتَٰبَ وَٱلۡحِكۡمَةَ
\AYAH_AR_END

\AYAH_START
Indeed, Allāh has done the believers a great favor by raising up among them a
Messenger from their own people, reciting His verses to them, purifying them,
and teaching them the Book and wisdom. (Sūrat Āl ʿImrān, Q3:164)
\AYAH_END

While explaining the meaning of _ḥikmah_, Abū Ḥayyān al-Andalusī
\hon{\M_HIM}{\BODY} writes that Mujāhid \hon{\M_HIM}{\BODY} said, "_Ḥikmah_
means understanding the Quran."[-@@bahr_muhit, vol. 1, p. 562]

Imam al-Ṭabarī \hon{\M_HIM}{\BODY} writes, "The correct view, in our opinion,
is that _ḥikmah_ is knowledge of Allāh's rulings, the understanding of which
cannot be attained except through the explanation of the Messenger."
[-@@tafsir_tabari, vol. 2, p. 577]

Imam al-Shāfiʿī \hon{\M_HIM}{\BODY} writes in _Al-Risālah_, "I heard those
scholars of the Quran whose knowledge I trust say, '_Ḥikmah_ refers to the
Sunnah of the Messenger of Allāh \hon{\PBUH}{\BODY}.'"[-@@risalah_shafi3, p. 78]

The role of this _ḥikmah_, namely the Sunnah, in understanding the Noble Quran
is so fundamental that Imam al-Shāṭibī \hon{\M_HIM}{\BODY} unequivocally states,
"No one is capable of understanding it except one who has devoted himself to
that which aids him in doing so, namely the Sunnah that explains the
Book."[-@@muwafaqat, vol. 4, p. 878]

Qāḍī Ibn al-ʿArabī al-Mālikī \hon{\M_HIM}{\BODY} goes even further, saying,
"Every expression in the Quran from which a legal ruling is derived is, in
itself, concise. Its explanation depends upon the statements of the Prophet
\hon{\PBUH}{\BODY}. Its detailed clarification must be sought within the
Sharīʿah, where it will certainly be found. If, hypothetically, no such
explanation existed, then the legal obligation arising from that ruling would
itself cease."[-@@ahkamQuranIbnArabi, vol. 1, p. 17]

These statements of the leading scholars of Islam clearly demonstrate just how
indispensable the Prophetic Sunnah is for attaining a sound understanding of the
Noble Quran.
