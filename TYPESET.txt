12 Principles for Understanding the Qur’an

The Qurʾān is the ultimate source of guidance, but understanding its message requires more than reading a translation. It demands sound principles, scholarly method, and a proper appreciation of the revelation.

In this important work, Shaykh al-Tafsīr Muḥammad Owais al-Nagrāmī al-Nadwī رحمه الله outlines twelve essential principles for understanding the Qurʾān correctly. From reverence for revelation and adherence to the Sunnah to Arabic linguistics, classical Tafsīr, and the limits of personal opinion, he lays the foundations for a disciplined and authentic approach to the Book of Allah.

At a time when Qurʾānic verses are often interpreted without proper knowledge or context, these principles are indispensable for distinguishing sound understanding from conjecture. A concise yet profound guide for students, researchers, and every sincere seeker wishing to approach the Qurʾān with greater clarity, depth, and understanding. Discover not merely what the Qurʾān says, but how to understand its message with the knowledge, method, and reverence it deserves.
